Theme: Frontier Observatory. Cold void, warm signal. The page lives in cool near-black space; the brand cream and vermillion are the human signal cutting through it. One dark theme, locked. One accent, locked.

Color

cream · text#F6F2EA
accent · brand#E5391B
accent-bright · buttons#F03E1F
accent-deep · glows only#7E1E0C

Usage: #E5391B is the brand accent for text, rules, and glows against the void. Button fills step up to #F03E1F so ink-on-accent labels pass WCAG AA. The deep tone is reserved for atmosphere (horizon glow, bars), never text.

Typography

Two families only. Archivo carries display (Expanded width) through body (normal width); IBM Plex Mono is reserved for data, figures, and captions. No uppercase eyebrow labels above headlines: the headline does the work.

section · 6-10rem

4px base scale. Sections breathe at 6 to 10rem (fluid). Density stays low: this is an observatory, not a cockpit.

Shape and components

Shape rule: surfaces are sharp (radius 0), interactive elements are pills (radius 999). No in-between radii anywhere.

Motion

Restrained and cinematic: content rises 20px on load with a 90ms stagger, stars drift imperceptibly, orbit rings rotate once per four minutes. Everything collapses to static under prefers-reduced-motion. Ease: cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1). Nothing loops for attention.
