# Share of Answer Report — Query Set v1 (for approval)

50 buying-intent prompts, 10 verticals × 5, phrased the way real people
ask AI assistants (not keyword-ese). Local verticals anchor to three
neutral metros (Denver, Charlotte, Austin) so results are comparable
across runs; national verticals stay unanchored.

Run protocol (per SEO-PLAN + AUTHORITY-PLAYBOOK §3): each query asked
verbatim, fresh session, no memory/personalization, across ChatGPT,
Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, and Google AI Overviews. Record:
brands named (in order) · sources cited (domain + type) · answer format.
Monthly cadence, same set, additions only in versioned batches.
