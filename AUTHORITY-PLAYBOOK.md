# Authority Playbook — Aivium Digital

Companion to SEO-PLAN.md. That plan builds pages; this one builds the
authority (DR, citations, entity trust) those pages need to rank. Three
plays plus the local layer. Written 2026-08-01.

## Scope: which brand does what (decided 2026-08-01, fact-checked)

**This entire playbook executes for ONE brand: Aivium Digital.** Not the
parent, not the other divisions. Why:

- **GBP:** multiple profiles from the same owner/address/phone with
  similar AI-ish categories is a textbook suspension pattern, and Google
  filters lookalike same-address listings so only one shows anyway.
  One profile: Aivium Digital.
- **Directories & reviews:** reviews are the currency and they don't
  transfer. Four Clutch profiles with two reviews each lose to one
  profile with eight. Every review ask routes to Aivium Digital.
- **Expert quotes:** Steven is one person; one byline ("founder & CEO of
  Aivium Digital"), always. Rotating brand attributions fragments the
  person-entity we're building.
- **Domains:** one strong domain beats several weak ones; search engines
  detect common ownership, and same-owner sites chasing the same terms
  split authority instead of multiplying it.

**Parent Aivium gets an entity footprint only** (no agency directories,
no GBP): LinkedIn company page, Crunchbase-type organization listings,
and the cross-site schema already live (parentOrganization /
subOrganization). Its job is to make Aivium Digital more credible, not
to compete with it for listings.

**Partners / Scale / Spark get NOTHING until launch.** When a division
is live with a genuinely distinct service, its own reviews, and ideally
its own phone/contact, it earns its own profiles then — distinctness is
what makes multi-brand legitimate. Premature duplication is what reads
as spam.

---

The one rule that makes all of this compound: **every listing, profile,
and pitch uses the canonical kit below, verbatim.** Consistent entity
data across the web is what teaches both Google and AI engines who
Aivium Digital is. Fifty slightly-different descriptions are fifty weak
signals; one description everywhere is one strong one.

## 3. Original research: "The Share of Answer Report" (the ceiling-raiser)

Why: the most-linked pages in this niche are stat roundups. Publish
proprietary numbers and you flip from citing to being cited — every
"AI search statistics" listicle becomes a potential backlink, and AI
engines quote the stats with your name attached.

**Minimum viable study (Claude executes the mechanics with you):**
1. **Query set:** 50 buying-intent prompts across 10 verticals
   (5 each: home services, legal, healthcare, e-commerce, SaaS,
   finance, restaurants, real estate, fitness, B2B services) — e.g.
   "best CRM for a small law firm", "who should I hire for a kitchen
   remodel in [metro]". I draft these; you approve.
2. **Engines:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Google
   AI Overviews. Same prompts, logged monthly.
3. **Record per answer:** brands named (order), sources cited
   (domain + type: directory/review site/brand site/press), answer
   format. A spreadsheet or JSON per run.
4. **Publish (post 1):** "We asked 6 AI engines 50 buying questions.
   Here's who they recommend and why." — headline stats shaped for
   lifting: "X% of recommendations cited a review site", "Directories
   appeared in X of 50 answers", "The same brand was named by 4+
   engines only X% of the time."
5. **Repeat monthly** → trend data nobody else has → the report
   becomes a franchise (and the exact methodology behind the client
   share-of-answer deliverable you already sell).

Costs almost nothing but time; run 1 can be collected in an evening.
This also feeds SEO-PLAN Phase 2 topic #1 ("Best AI visibility tools")
with first-hand data instead of a me-too listicle.

## 4. Local layer: Google Business Profile + Bing Places

**Decisions first (only you can make these):**
- Address vs Service-Area Business (SAB). If you don't want the office
  address public, choose SAB: address hidden, service area set to your
  metro + United States. This is normal for agencies.
- The name must be exactly "Aivium Digital" (no keyword stuffing —
  suspension risk).

**GBP steps (~20 min + verification wait):**
1. google.com/business → sign in with the business Google account.
2. Add business: "Aivium Digital" → Primary category: **Marketing
   agency** (add secondary: Internet marketing service, Marketing
   consultant).
3. SAB setup: hide address, set service area.
4. Phone 240-730-4333 · site https://aiviumdigital.com (exact NAP).
5. Verification: video verification is standard for SABs now — Google
   will ask for a short screen/office recording. Do it same-day; queues
   grow.
6. After verify: 50-word kit description in the About field, services
   list from the kit, logo + 3-4 brand images (hero bands work),
   first Google Post = the Signals launch post link.
7. Reviews: same ask as Clutch but Google link for local-intent
   clients. Never incentivize; just make the link easy.
8. **Bing Places** (bingplaces.com): "Import from Google Business
   Profile" — one click after GBP exists. Bing feeds ChatGPT/Copilot
   local answers, so this one disproportionately matters for GEO.
