## 7. SERP Reality Notes (for copywriters)

- **AI Overviews appear on virtually every SERP in this niche.** Open every page and post with a direct, quotable answer to the target query (1–2 sentence definition style) — this is what earns AIO/LLM citations.
- **Winnable now:** `generative engine optimization agency` (DR 36/44 hold top spots), `aeo services` (DR 37–55), `ai automation agency` (DR 9 site at #2; page 1 is mostly Reddit/Quora/YouTube), `ai consulting for small businesses` (DR 7–9 sites on page 1).
- **Harder but crackable:** `ai seo agency` / `ai seo services` — DR 80+ at top, DR 23 at #9. Page 1 entry realistic; top 3 is a 12+ month play.
- **Don't lead with:** anything in the P3 table — DR 70–90+ sites own those SERPs.
- **Structural pattern that wins:** dedicated service pages with anchor-linked sections ("What is GEO?", "Our GEO system", "What a GEO agency does") earning sitelinks; cost-transparent sections for SMB terms; a single service page can hold an entire keyword cluster (The AdFirm's one /ai-seo/ page ranks for three P1 terms).
- **Window is closing:** KD 5–20 terms with $12–20 CPCs (especially GEO/AEO agency terms) will harden as more agencies launch dedicated pages. Ship the two service pages and blog topics 1–4 first.
